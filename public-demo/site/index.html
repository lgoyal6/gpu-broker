This explorer reads 32 recorded CPU simulations, seed 42. Capacity, arrivals, prices and power are modelled; GB carbon data is historical. Dollars and emissions are estimates, not measured savings. Nothing launches, submits a job, or connects to AWS or a database.

Choose the tradeoff

Queue time and fairness can move in opposite directions. A policy named “lowest-carbon” scores placement candidates; it does not guarantee the lowest aggregate emissions over a full workload.

Native source, recording and limits

The results come from the repository's existing gpubroker sim command, which drives the production policy.Decide logic. This browser selects recorded results; it does not reimplement the scheduler or evaluate arbitrary jobs. No credentials, job submission, database or cloud worker exists in this demo.
